About Hope Haven

Hope Haven Youth Shelter is a registered non-profit organisation working with young people aged 16 to 24 who have nowhere safe to sleep. We combine immediate shelter with the longer-term support that makes independence possible.

Our history

Hope Haven was founded in 2018 by a group of social workers and community volunteers who saw how few emergency beds existed for young adults who had aged out of child-care facilities. What began as an eight-bed house has grown into two shelters, a counselling practice and a vocational training programme.

Mission

To break the cycle of youth homelessness through safe housing and community support.

Vision

A community where every young person has a safe home and a viable future.

Values

Dignity, safety, honesty and youth participation in every decision we make.

Who we serve

Our audience includes local donors, corporate sponsors, community volunteers and - most importantly - young people seeking support services. Every page on this website is written so that a young person in crisis can find help within one click.
