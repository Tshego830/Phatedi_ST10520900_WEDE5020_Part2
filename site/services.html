Our services

All Hope Haven services are free to the young people who use them. Intake is available 24 hours a day at both shelter sites, and no young person is turned away without a referral to safe accommodation.

Core programmes

1. Emergency shelter

48 beds across two sites, with a hot meal, shower, clean bedding and a locked storage space on the first night. Average stay is 21 days while a longer-term plan is put in place.

2. Counselling & mental health

Weekly one-on-one sessions with a social worker, trauma-informed group therapy, and substance-use support through partner clinics.

3. Vocational training

Accredited short courses in carpentry, hospitality and computer literacy, plus CV writing and interview preparation.

4. Education support

Saturday tutoring, matric re-write assistance and bursary applications with our education partners.

5. Family reunification

Mediation and home assessments so that young people can return home safely where that is the right outcome.

6. Aftercare & independent living

Six months of follow-up support after a resident moves out, including rent deposit assistance and monthly check-ins.

How intake works

Step What happens Timing
1. Reach out Walk in, call the 24-hour line, or complete our contact form. Any time
2. Safety check A staff member confirms immediate needs and bed availability. Within 1 hour
3. Intake interview A social worker records your history and consent. Within 24 hours
4. Support plan Counselling, education and skills goals are agreed with you. Within 7 days

Skills that lead to work

168 residents moved into employment or further study in the past year through our vocational programmes.

Outreach in the community

Our street outreach team visits three neighbourhoods each week with food, blankets and referrals.
