Every young person deserves a safe home

Since 2018 Hope Haven has offered emergency housing, counselling and vocational training to young people aged 16 to 24 - breaking the cycle of youth homelessness one life at a time.

How we help

Emergency housing

A warm bed, meals and a safe space on the same night a young person reaches out - no referral needed.

Counselling & wellbeing

One-on-one and group counselling with qualified social workers, plus family reunification support where it is safe.

Vocational training

Skills programmes, CV workshops and employer partnerships that help residents move into work and independent living.
